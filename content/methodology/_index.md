WELP is currently undergoing a methodology revision.

Updated documentation will be published once the revised evaluation framework has been validated.

Previous evaluations and their original evidence remain available as historical research. Differences in methodology, hardware, runtime, and configuration should be considered when comparing results. Earlier results are not treated as invalid, and they have not necessarily been retested under the forthcoming methodology.

[Evaluations](/evaluations/) keeps the model catalog and original evidence links. [Research](/records/) keeps technical reports and investigations.
