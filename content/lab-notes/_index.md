Lab Notes are the working record for WumboLabs.

Projects describe what exists. Benchmarks capture measured test results. Lab Notes explain what changed, what broke, what was learned, and why certain decisions were made.

Good notes here are short, dated, and tied to real work:

- project change logs
- design and architecture decisions
- troubleshooting notes
- experiment summaries
- website and documentation updates
- future-me reminders worth keeping outside a chat thread

These are public field notes, not private scratchpads or full documentation dumps.
