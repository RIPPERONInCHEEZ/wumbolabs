This route is kept for compatibility with earlier links and the Lab Records publication pipeline. It is no longer a primary browsing surface.

- **Browse evidence:** the [Lab Records](/records/) index is the canonical evidence archive. It lists the evaluation catalog and all published technical records.
- **How evidence is produced:** [Methodology](/methodology/) explains WELP, evidence requirements, review and scoring, claim boundaries, and the publication workflow.

Individual Lab Record pages under `/labs/` remain published. Each maps to canonical public evidence — or an explicit evidence-pending state — and is listed in the Lab Records evaluation catalog.
